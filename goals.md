
The goal is not to create perpetual security vaults of communication, but to buy us time in the rising tide of global surveillance.

It's tactical, not perfectly secure. The goal is create barrier not to create perfect softwares.

Alpha-quality cryptography is acceptable for stakes of same level. Any tactical advantage counts, and matters.

There is a tension between privacy and trust. Reusing one public key makes a
person's activity linkable; using unrelated keys with no verifiable connection
to a trusted root weakens the purpose of PKI. The goal is to preserve
verifiable provenance with minimal exposure of linkability.

## Necessary equivalents for distributed system

The main goal at foremost, is not a privacy-obsession, but necessary equivalent components that faciliate the use of a distributed infrastructure without it breaking down by spam or targeted attacks.
